## NDSA Digital Preservation 2026 Conference

The [call for proposals](https://www.ndsa.org/conference/digital-preservation-virtual-2026/cfp/) for the upcoming annual NDSA Digital Preservation conference is now open. The conference will be held virtually on November 4-5, 2026\. The deadline to [submit a proposal](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScl41ofGogIb1FCs1ZIPnb9ENC2-gxpqFWBuiJs65NSL5rDbQ/viewform?usp=sharing&ouid=118333939818000509796) is July 1, 2026 with submitters notified of acceptance starting in late July.

## Organizational Transition Updates

Work to transition into a formal nonprofit continues in partnership with our colleagues at NISO. The NDSA trademark is now actively in the process of renewal in partnership with NISO, with paperwork filed with the U.S. Trademark Office. Specific artifacts necessary for incorporation are in various stages of completion. Articles of Incorporation have been drafted and the Coordinating Committee has voted to adopt these, which can now permit the registration process to begin. A Conflict of Interest policy is currently in the final stages of being drafted and will be voted on in the near future. Finally, Bylaws are in active discussion by the Leadership Team to ensure we adopt a set that will satisfy NDSA’s needs.

## Interest and Working Group Updates

**Working Groups**

* The [Conference Planning Working Group](https://www.ndsa.org/conference/) has begun coordinating with the Events Steering Committee and is working to finalize details related to registration costs and sponsorship tiers.  
* The Events Steering Committee continues to recruit members and now has a [page on the NDSA website](https://www.ndsa.org/groups/events-steering/) where more information can be found..  
* The Excellence Awards Working Group held a subgroup meeting on May 28, 2026 to discuss award categories and submitted recommendations back to the full working group.  
* The Fixity Survey Working Group has [published the 2025 NDSA Fixity Survey Report](https://www.ndsa.org/2026/04/30/2025-ndsa-fixity-survey-report-published.html).  
* The Membership Working Group is working on updating the member application and refining the application review process.   
* The Staffing Survey Working Group is finishing the editing process for the report and will be submitting to the Communications and Publications Working Group soon.  
* The Web Archiving Survey Working Group has completed updating the survey, including revisions and new questions, and [the survey is now open through June 24 for submissions](https://www.ndsa.org/2026/06/03/it's-here-the-2026-ndsa-web-archiving-survey.html). We welcome responses from NDSA members and the broader web archiving community.
