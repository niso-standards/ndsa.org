As a reminder, NDSA is publishing monthly posts summarizing the activities taken by the Leadership Team as well as highlighting past and upcoming activities, which can also be found on the [calendar](https://ndsa.org/events/calendar/). If you are looking to catch up, you can read the [March update](https://www.ndsa.org/2026/04/09/march-2026-ndsa-recap.html).

NDSA is working to keep the listserv current with members and as a result, email addresses which bounce back will be removed from the list. You can always [sign-up for the updated list](https://gaggle.email/join/ndsalist@ndsa.org) at any time with your current email address, you must also include both your first and last name to be approved. NDSA also has Slack available to members. [Sign-up](https://ndsa2.slack.com/) to view channels devoted to specific interest groups or job announcements.

## Organizational Transition Updates

### Website update
Updates to the NDSA website continue. If you see any broken links or other errors, please report those to the chairs of the [Communications and Publications Working Group](https://ndsa.org/groups/communications-publications/).

### Nonprofit Incorporation
Work to transition into a formal nonprofit continues in partnership with our colleagues at NISO. A consulting organization has been identified to assist with the process, including creating articles of incorporation, bylaws, registering with the IRS, and forming a formal board with officers. NISO is also assisting with the process of renewing the NDSA trademark.

## Interest and Working Group Updates

**Interest Groups**
* The Standards and Practices Interest Group held a meeting on April 20, 2026\. Julie McVey, National Geographic Society and a founding member of the [Digital Object Authenticity Working Group](https://www.doawg.org/), spoke about the work of DOAWG which continued discussions about content authenticity and provenance in the context of AI. 

**Working Groups**
* The [Conference Planning Working Group](https://www.ndsa.org/conference/) held a meeting on April 20, 2026\. The group is working to finalize conference dates, finish assigning co-chairs to subgroups, and determining the conference theme with a call for proposals to be distributed in early May.  
* The Climate Watch Working Group has onboarded new members and has published their [January to March 2026 newsletter](https://ndsaclimatewatch.substack.com/p/january-to-march-2026).  
* The Events Steering Committee has finalized their charge which has been approved by the Coordinating Committee, and is now recruiting committee members and preparing for future conference planning.  
* The Excellence Awards Working Group continues to make progress on reviewing the awards categories to determine whether they could benefit from an update. This discussion was continued in a meeting on April 30, 2026 and subgroups continue to push the work forward.  
* The Fixity Survey Working Group has [published the 2025 NDSA Fixity Survey Report](https://www.ndsa.org/2026/04/30/2025-ndsa-fixity-survey-report-published.html).  
* The Membership Working Group held a New Member Orientation on April 23, 2026 to welcome and onboard the newest member organizations, as well as existing or prospective members.   
* The Staffing Survey Working Group is continuing to draft the survey report.  
* The Web Archiving Survey Working Group is actively working to finish drafting the new survey.
