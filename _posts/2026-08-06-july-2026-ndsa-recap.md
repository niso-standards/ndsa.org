## NDSA Digital Preservation 2026 Conference

The call for proposals for the upcoming annual [NDSA Digital Preservation conference](https://www.ndsa.org/conference/) has now closed with 58 submissions received. Proposal review by the planning committee is underway and will conclude on August 5\. The conference will be held virtually on November 4-5, 2026\. Submitters will be notified of acceptance starting in mid-August, and registration for the conference will open on August 16\. The planning committee would urge anyone with an idea for a keynote speaker to [submit a nomination](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeIBBe3IcBz_pnjLuuOao6ughwpPzSS_wQMs9qJlaUIChcCLA/viewform).

## Organizational Transition Updates

In July, the main focus for organizational transition was continuing to craft a cohesive set of Bylaws that will serve the needs of NDSA as an organization. This has entailed discussion and education about the role NDSA plays with the partner organizations assisting with this process.

## Interest and Working Group Updates

**Working Groups**

* The Climate Watch Working Group is in the process of drafting their next newsletter.  
* The Communications and Publications Working Group is working to review and update the style guide and publishing template used by all NDSA publications.  
* The Events Steering Committee is finalizing their membership and are in the process of developing a kick-off plan.  
* The Excellence Awards Working Group shared a proposed update to the award categories with NDSA Leadership which was accepted, with work to update the submission and judging process now underway.  
* The Staffing Survey Working Group is in the final stages of editing this report.  
* The Web Archiving Survey closed with 190 responses and the Web Archiving Survey Working Group is now working to review the data.
