title: "NDSA Publications moving to Zenodo"
date: '2026-09-04 12:45:03 +0000'
---

For many years NDSA has been publishing our reports and other materials in OSF.  OSF recently announced that they would [no longer be accepting new content for publishing](https://help.osf.io/article/727-osf-projects-transition?hsCtaTracking=a08cf360-121d-40fa-9c35-7291e86c0e05%7C0238d20a-8b54-470d-844c-aa3c78d9e808); this follows their announcement of [no longer supporting OSF Meetings](https://help.osf.io/article/727-osf-projects-transition?hsCtaTracking=a08cf360-121d-40fa-9c35-7291e86c0e05%7C0238d20a-8b54-470d-844c-aa3c78d9e808) last year. Due to these changes, NDSA investigated several options and has decided to use Zenodo for our publications. The digital preservation community already has a significant presence in Zenodo and it has become the repository for [iPRES content since 2024](https://zenodo.org/communities-search?q=ipres&l=list&p=1&s=10&sort=bestmatch). All new NDSA publications will be uploaded to Zenodo and past NDSA content will be migrated to Zenodo over the next few months. 

Bookmark the [NDSA Community in Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/ndsa/records?q=&l=list&p=1&s=10&sort=newest) to find our publications.  While it looks sparse now, expect more content to be added in the coming weeks!
